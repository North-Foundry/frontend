## What Sticko can access

**Reminders and calendars.** If you add a reminders or calendar sticker and give permission, Sticko
reads your reminders and events to draw them on your board. It reads them on your device only and
never changes them. You can turn this access off at any time in **Settings › Privacy & Security ›
Reminders** or **Calendars**.

**Photos.** When you tap **Save to Photos**, Sticko adds the board image to your photo library. It
asks only for permission to add photos, so it cannot see the photos you already have.

## What Sticko stores

Your boards, their text and your preferences, such as the sticker font and size, are stored in the
app's own storage on your iPhone. They are included in your normal device backups, which Apple
handles according to your iCloud settings and Apple's privacy policy.

## What leaves your device

Nothing. Sticko has no network code and sends no data to North Foundry or to anyone else, so it
shares no data with third parties.

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may send us
anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. Apple collects these under its own privacy policy, and
they do not identify you.

## Keeping and deleting your data

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to keep or delete. To remove what Sticko
stores on your iPhone, delete a board in the app, or delete the app to remove everything.

## Children

Sticko collects no data from anyone, children included.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws, you can ask what personal data we hold about you and have it
corrected or erased. For Sticko the answer is none, but you can still write to us with any question
about your privacy. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority
where you live or work.

## Changes to this policy

If Sticko changes how it handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top before the
new version of the app is released.
